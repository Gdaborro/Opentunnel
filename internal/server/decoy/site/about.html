About Aborro Systems

Aborro Systems started in 2019 with four second-hand servers in a rented cage at a Brisbane data centre and one customer (a friend's accounting firm that needed somewhere to run its practice software off-site). The cage is now two rows of racks, the friend is still a customer, and we've been in our own Virginia facility since 2023.

We're deliberately small. Twelve racks, two uplink providers, one NOC. That scale is the point: every customer's gear is something we've physically installed, and when we say we'll reboot your server we mean one of us will walk twenty metres and do it.
